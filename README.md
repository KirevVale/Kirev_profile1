# KirevOfProfile

A personal **osu!standard** skin for osu!lazer, focused on readability, consistent input and a black/pink/cyan visual identity.

**Credits & disclaimer:** Most visual and audio assets were created for KirevOfProfile. Some D.Va / Overwatch voice clips are third-party material and remain the property of their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by osu! or Blizzard Entertainment. Reuse of third-party material is subject to its original rights.

## Personal Setup

Reference configuration for **1920×1080 / 165 Hz**, keyboard and mouse, primarily **No Mod** with occasional **Hidden**. Priorities: readability, consistent timing and minimal distractions.
